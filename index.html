Medicheck Pharma Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

In short: Medicheck Pharma works completely offline. Everything you enter stays on your own device. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics.

This policy explains how the Medicheck Pharma Android app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Medicheckco ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app has no internet access (it does not request the Android internet permission), has no user accounts on our side, and contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

2. Information you store in the app

To run your pharmacy, you may enter business and personal information into the app, for example:

  • Your pharmacy's details, staff names and PINs
  • Customer and patient details such as name, phone number, email, address, allergies, insurance details and prescriptions
  • Supplier details, medicines, stock, sales, purchases, payments and expenses

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it or access it. You, as the pharmacy, are responsible for this information and for following the privacy and health-record laws that apply where you work, including getting any consent your patients' data requires.

Staff PINs are never stored as plain text. They are protected with a salted one-way hash (PBKDF2).

3. Device permissions

  • Camera (optional): used only to scan barcodes. Images are processed on the device, are not saved and are never sent anywhere. The app works without this permission; you can type or use a USB/Bluetooth scanner instead.
  • Notifications (optional): used to show alerts on this device, such as stock and expiry reminders.

4. Sharing that you choose

The app shares information only when you tap a button to do so, and only with the app or service you pick:

  • Sending a receipt or message by WhatsApp or SMS opens that app with the text ready; you decide whether to send it.
  • Sharing or printing a receipt or report (PDF or CSV) passes the file to the app or printer you choose.
  • Exporting a backup saves a copy of your data to the location you choose, such as a memory card, USB drive or cloud storage app.
  • Contacting us or reporting a bug from Help & feedback opens your email app with your message ready; you decide whether to send it. If you leave the option ticked, it adds the app version, phone model, Android version, trial or full-version status and recent technical error messages. It never includes your pharmacy, patient or sales records. We use what you send only to reply and fix problems.

Once you share information with another app or service, that app's own privacy policy applies.

5. Free trial and purchase

The app includes a 30-day free trial, after which the full version can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. The trial start date is stored only on your device. Purchases are made and processed by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; we do not receive or store your payment details. The app only asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether the full version has been bought.

6. Backups

The app makes an automatic backup copy of your data in its own folder on your device every day and keeps the number of copies you set. These copies stay on the device. The app does not use Android's cloud backup, so your data is not uploaded to Google Drive automatically. If you export a backup yourself, keep that file safe, because it contains all your pharmacy's data.

7. Keeping your data safe

Because your data stays on your device, its safety depends on the device too. We recommend using a screen lock on the device, giving each staff member their own PIN, and keeping exported backup files in a secure place.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete records in the app, and you can remove all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling also removes the automatic backups kept in the app's folder. Exported backup files you saved elsewhere are not removed and must be deleted by you.

9. Children

The app is a business tool for pharmacies and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Not medical advice

Medicheck Pharma is pharmacy management software. Allergy and expiry warnings are aids only and do not replace professional judgement. The app does not give medical advice and does not sell medicines.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, we will update this policy before releasing that version.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at medistock.us@gmail.com.
